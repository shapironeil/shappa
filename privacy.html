Privacy Policy - Shappa

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Shappa ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data We Collect

When you authorize Shappa to access your eBay account, we collect:

  • Your eBay user information
  • Your listings and inventory data

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Manage your eBay listings
  • Synchronize inventory across platforms
  • Process orders and fulfillment

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
